Career Goal Map Worksheet

Connect your long-term vision, your WHY, two or three medium-term goals, the people who can help, and the next actions you can actually finish. This becomes a working document you return to, so you can check progress, celebrate wins, and adjust.

A small map you will actually use is worth more than a big one you won't.

Long-Term Goal

Your North Star (5-10 years)

Write a short narrative (150-250 words) describing your professional life five to ten years from now.

Start from your "No-Fail" vision from last week and revise it. You do not need to begin from scratch. Touch on the points that matter most to you:

  • What you want to be doing professionally during a typical week
  • Where you want to be living and who you want to be working with or for
  • What kind of income and stability you're working toward
  • What impact you want your work to have, and what success looks like for you

This is your North Star, not your daily scorecard. It will evolve as you gain experience.

Medium-Term Goals

Backward Planning (1-3 years)

Identify two or three medium-term goals by planning backward from your long-term vision.

Use the "+ Add Another Medium-Term Goal" button to add your second and third goal. For each goal, state it in SMART format, explain in a sentence or two how it connects to your vision and your WHY, and choose the two helpers who matter most.

Three goals is the maximum for this map.
4

Short-Term Action Steps

Next 1-3 Months

For each medium-term goal, identify two actions you can complete in the next one to three months.

Make each one a specific, concrete task where you can clearly tell whether you did it, and where success is genuinely achievable. Label each action with the WIN it creates.
